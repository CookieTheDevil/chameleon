Chameleon

The game where you blend in, or try to find the one who doesn’t.

Rules

The Chameleon must blend in and figure out the secret word, while the other players try to identify the Chameleon without revealing it.

Go around the group 1-3 times. On each turn, every player says one word loosely related to the secret word. Be careful: Not too obvious, but not too suspicious!

When the discussion ends, vote for who you think the Chameleon is.

If the vote is wrong, the Chameleon wins immediately. If the Chameleon is caught, they get one final chance to guess the secret word. A correct guess means the Chameleon wins; a wrong guess means everyone else wins.
